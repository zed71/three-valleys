What the guide is

The extension helps you research public tasting rooms in Walla Walla Valley and The Rocks District; Santa Barbara nested AVAs; and nested Willamette AVAs. It is an informational trip-planning tool. It is not a booking agency, not a winery, and not affiliated with the businesses listed.

Accuracy of listings

Hours, tasting fees, visit policies, and reservation links change. Unpublished fees are shown as “Not published — confirm with winery.” Always confirm details with the winery before you visit. We do not invent tasting fees.
